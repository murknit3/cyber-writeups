# Digital Forensics & Incident Response

This section contains investigation-focused notes and write-ups covering endpoint, Windows, Splunk and network evidence.

## Areas covered

- **Splunk investigations** — searching, pivoting and correlating events.
- **Windows Event Logs** — authentication, process execution and security-relevant telemetry.
- **Network forensics** — packet analysis, suspicious connections and protocol-level evidence.

The goal is to document the investigation process, not just the final answer: what evidence was available, which hypothesis was tested, what query or filter was used, and what the evidence showed.
